Topology and Policy Scenario

You are configuring R1 at the main campus. GigabitEthernet0/0/0 faces the 20 Mbps wide area link to the instructional site, and that link is the congestion point. Interactive business traffic arrives already marked AF31 by the access switch, which is the trust boundary. Voice media arrives unmarked from a handset outside that boundary and must be identified by its transport ports.

The policy, named WAN-EDGE, must give voice a strict priority queue bounded at 10 percent of the link and guarantee interactive business traffic 30 percent. Build it in the three stages the configuration model requires: identify the traffic, state what happens to each class, then attach the result to the interface.

Enter commands exactly as you would at the R1 command line. Keyword abbreviations that Cisco IOS accepts are also accepted here.
