Module 11 Knowledge Checker: Network Troubleshooting

This ungraded formative activity verifies your understanding of scope, the diagnostic approaches, and the fault signatures introduced across this course before you attempt the formative practice activities. Answer all ten questions and select Submit Assessment. Return to the module page and launch the Command Syntax Checker once you have achieved a score of ten out of ten.

1. A ticket reads only that the third floor cannot reach the records server. What is the correct first action?

2. One workstation cannot reach anything, while the workstation beside it works normally. Where does the investigation belong?

3. Users on every floor report that one particular server is unreachable, while everything else works. What does this scope indicate?

4. Why must a change that did not resolve the symptom be reversed before the next change is tried?

5. A technician applies a correction that is certainly right, and the symptom does not improve. What should be considered?

6. Forty users across three floors lose connectivity at the same moment. Which approach fits the evidence?

7. The evidence points nowhere in particular. Which approach is the sensible default?

8. An access control list appears correct, and its match counters remain at zero throughout a connectivity test. What does this evidence point at?

9. A translation configuration looks correct, traffic has been sent, and the translation table is empty. What is the most likely cause?

10. Why is reading the running configuration back an inadequate way to confirm that a feature works?
